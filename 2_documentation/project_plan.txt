PROJECT TITLE:
E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

BUSINESS PROBLEM:
Analyze e-commerce sales, customer behavior, product performance,
seller performance, payments, reviews, and delivery operations to
identify trends, strengths, and areas for business improvement.

TOOLS:
SQL
Python / Pandas
Excel
Power BI

MAIN KPIs:
1. Total Revenue
2. Total Orders
3. Total Customers
4. Average Order Value
5. Repeat Customer Rate
6. Average Review Score
7. Average Delivery Time
8. Late Delivery Rate

MAIN ANALYSIS QUESTIONS:
1. How have orders and revenue changed over time?
2. Which product categories generate the most revenue?
3. Which locations generate the most sales?
4. How many customers make repeat purchases?
5. What is the average order value?
6. Which payment methods are most commonly used?
7. Which sellers generate the most revenue?
8. What is the average delivery time?
9. Do late deliveries receive lower review scores?
10. Which categories have the best and worst customer reviews?
11. Which months have the highest demand?
12. What areas of the business need improvement?
